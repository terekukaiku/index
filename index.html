A Letter From My Heart

The things I'm not great at saying out loud.

💗

Dear Didi,

I don't say this nearly enough, so I'm writing it here where it can't be laughed off: thank you. Thank you for every laugh, every scolding, every piece of advice, and every time you stood by me when I hadn't earned it.

People say family is blood. I never believed it, and you're the reason. You didn't have to care, but you did, on my best days and my worst. There's a kind of love that isn't written in any family tree, the kind that chooses you. That's what you gave me.

I remember the little things most. The way you'd notice when I was quiet, the extra bite you'd leave for me, the "sab theek hai na?" you'd ask with just your eyes. You never made a big deal of it, and that's exactly why it meant so much.

You taught me things without trying: how to be brave when you're scared, how to be kind when it's hard, how to laugh at yourself, and how to keep going even when the day is heavy. I still learn from you every single day.

I know you carry a lot. You show up for everyone, you smile through tired days, and you rarely ask for anything. So let me say this clearly: you're allowed to rest, to be soft, to not have it all figured out. I've got your back, always.

Life will get busy. We'll be in different cities, on different schedules, maybe in different time zones. None of it will change a thing. Whatever you're chasing, whatever scares you, I'm in your corner, and you'll hear me the loudest.

One day I hope to be for you even half of what you've been for me. Till then, please keep being exactly who you are: funny, fierce, kind, dramatic and completely irreplaceable.

You're not just a senior I admire. You're my Didi, my guide, my family, and one of the best parts of my story.

With all my love and respect,
Your little brother 💛
